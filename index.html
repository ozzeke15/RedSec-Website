03 / Our approach

A clear process.
From scope to retest.

01 / Define

Start with the boundaries.

Define the assets, objectives, authorised techniques and testing window. Agree on operational boundaries and communication before testing begins.

02 / Understand

Keep the context.

Assess the agreed scope and validate findings with reproducible evidence. Explain the impact, affected assets and limits of what was tested.

03 / Improve

Make the next step clear.

Deliver prioritised findings and practical remediation guidance. Agree on retesting to check whether the reported weaknesses have been resolved.
