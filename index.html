02 / Pentesting

Find the weakness.
Prove the impact.

A penetration test examines how weaknesses in your applications or infrastructure could be exploited, within a scope agreed with your team.

01

Applications

Web applications & APIs

Review authentication, access controls, input handling and application logic. Investigate how weaknesses could expose data or allow unauthorised actions.

02

Infrastructure

External & internal environments

Assess exposed services, configurations and access boundaries. Validate potential attack paths and explain the systems and operations they could affect.

03

Evidence & remediation

A practical deliverable

Receive reproducible findings, an explanation of impact and prioritised recommendations. Agree on retesting to verify the fixes.

03 / Validation

Would your team
see it happen?

Test how your security controls and monitoring respond to agreed attacker behaviours. Connect offensive testing with the people responsible for detection and response.

01

Detection coverage

Telemetry & alerting

Check which events reach your security tooling, which alerts fire and where visibility is missing. Document the evidence behind each observation.

02

Purple teaming

Work with your defenders

Review controlled test scenarios together. Use the results to refine detection rules, investigation context and response procedures.

03

Verify improvement

Repeat & compare

Repeat the agreed scenarios after changes to check whether coverage and response have improved, and record the gaps that remain.

04 / Our approach

A clear process.
From scope to retest.

01 / Define

Start with the boundaries.

Define the assets, objectives, authorised techniques and testing window. Agree on operational boundaries and communication before testing begins.

02 / Understand

Keep the context.

Assess the agreed scope and validate findings with reproducible evidence. Explain the impact, affected assets and limits of what was tested.

03 / Improve

Make the next step clear.

Deliver prioritised findings and practical remediation guidance. Agree on retesting to check whether the reported weaknesses have been resolved.
